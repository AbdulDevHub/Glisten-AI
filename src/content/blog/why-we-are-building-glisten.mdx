Most UI problems aren't mysteries. Spacing drifts, contrast slips below what people can read, two buttons fight for attention. A good designer spots them in seconds. The trouble is that most people building interfaces don't have a good designer looking over their shoulder.

Glisten is an attempt to give them one.

## Two tools, one loop

**The critic** reviews a screenshot or a live URL and pins each problem where it happens, with a severity, an explanation and a suggested fix. It's built to be specific: "the card padding is 24px on the left and 16px on the right" is useful, while "improve the spacing" isn't.

**The generator** writes React components styled with Tailwind CSS from a prompt, a reference image or a critique. It streams the code with a live preview, and you refine it in chat.

**Fix with AI** connects the two: send a critique to the generator and get code that addresses each pinned issue.

## Where things stand

Glisten is in development, and this site is the first piece. Over the next few releases we're adding accounts, the critic, the generator, billing and team workspaces, in that order. The [changelog](/changelog) records each step as it ships.

## What we won't do

We won't put fake logos or made-up testimonials on this site. Until real people use Glisten, the [use cases](/use-cases) are labelled as examples. And we'll be upfront about limits: an AI critic can be wrong, and the docs say so.
