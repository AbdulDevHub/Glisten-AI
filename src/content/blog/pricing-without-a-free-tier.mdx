Plenty of AI tools offer a generous free tier, then quietly cut it back once the bills arrive. We'd rather start with pricing we can keep.

## Every run costs money

Each critique sends an image to a large vision model, and each generation asks a model to write and revise code. Those calls are billed per request by the AI provider. A free hosted tier means someone pays for them, and usually that ends in rate limits, worse models or a pricing change.

## So there are two ways to use Glisten

**Bring your own key (free).** Add your own Claude or OpenAI API key and you pay your provider directly, at their prices. We don't mark it up. Your key is encrypted at rest and never logged.

**Pro ($12/month) or Team ($29/seat/month).** We run the models for you. Pro includes 200 credits a month; Team pools 200 credits per seat. A critique costs 1 credit, a generation 2 and a chat tweak 1. Max quality runs on Claude Opus and costs double.

## Fair by design

- Credits are deducted when a run starts and **refunded automatically if it fails**.
- You see the cost of an action before you run it.
- Monthly credits don't roll over, which keeps the plan price low. Top-up packs cover busy months.

The full breakdown is on the [pricing page](/pricing), and the [credits docs](/docs/credits) cover the details.
