## What Glisten is

Glisten AI is an AI design critic and UI generator. The critic reviews a screenshot or a live page and pins each problem where it happens; the generator writes React and Tailwind code, including code that fixes what the critic found.

## Who builds it

Glisten is built by [Abdul Khan](https://github.com/AbdulDevHub), an independent developer. It started as a design-course project, a dark and animated landing page, and is growing into the product that page describes.

## How we work

- **Honest copy.** No invented customers, logos, testimonials or integrations. When something is planned rather than shipped, we say so.
- **Pricing we can keep.** Free with your own API key, or a flat plan with hosted credits. There's no free tier that might disappear later. [Read why](/blog/pricing-without-a-free-tier).
- **Your work is yours.** Uploads stay private to you or your team, we don't train models on them, and the code you generate belongs to you.
- **Built in the open.** Every release goes in the [changelog](/changelog).
