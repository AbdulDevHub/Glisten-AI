We want you to pay only for something that works for you. Here's how refunds work.

## Failed runs

If a hosted critique or generation fails on our side, its credits are returned to your balance automatically. You don't need to ask.

## New subscriptions

If you subscribe to Pro or Team and it isn't what you expected, ask for a refund within **14 days** of your first payment. We'll refund it in full as long as you've used fewer than 20 credits.

## Renewals and cancellations

- You can cancel at any time in billing settings. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.
- We don't refund partial months or unused credits after a renewal, except where the law requires it.
- If you were charged for a renewal you didn't expect, contact us within 7 days and we'll sort it out.

## Top-up packs

Unused top-up credits can be refunded within 14 days of purchase.

## Service problems

If an outage or bug on our side stops you using a paid plan for a significant time, contact us and we'll offer credits or a partial refund.

## BYOK usage

On the BYOK plan, AI usage is billed by your own provider (Anthropic or OpenAI). We can't refund those charges; contact your provider.

## How to ask

Use the [contact page](/contact) and choose "Billing". Approved refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days.
